## PlanBridge Pro

Pro provides unlimited checks of supplied trip bookings and on-device voice questions where supported. Monthly and annual subscriptions provide the same features. The App Store shows the price in your region and the billing period before you confirm a purchase. Annual subscriptions are charged for the full year upfront. No free trial is currently offered.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Cancellation stops future renewal; access normally continues for the paid period. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple at [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account to recover an active subscription. Privacy controls, export and data deletion do not require Pro.

## What the app can do

PlanBridge checks information you supply and selected calendar events. It does not guarantee a complete, feasible or current itinerary. Confirm important details with the relevant provider. Buffers are preferences and route times are estimates. The app does not make, amend or cancel bookings. Airlines, hotels, restaurants, rail operators and email providers are not connected, and live disruptions are not monitored. Answers are generated locally from available plan information.
