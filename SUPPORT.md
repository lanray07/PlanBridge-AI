# PlanBridge AI support

PlanBridge AI helps compare selected calendars and supplied bookings for possible timing conflicts. This repository currently contains a development version; no App Store release is available yet.

For help or bug reports, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/lanray07/PlanBridge-AI/issues). Include the app version, iOS version, what you expected and steps to reproduce using fictional plans. GitHub issues are public: do not include booking references, itineraries, calendar exports, addresses, payment information or other personal data.

## Common questions

- **Calendar plans missing:** grant calendar access in iOS Settings, select calendars in PlanBridge, then reopen the app to refresh.
- **Import rejected:** review the error and use a supported non-recurring ICS event or structured itinerary. Imports require confirmation before saving.
- **Voice unavailable:** type your question. Voice requires permissions and on-device speech recognition support for the device and language.
- **Missing delays or cancellations:** PlanBridge has no live airline, rail, hotel, restaurant or email connection. Confirm details with your provider.
- **Delete or export plans:** use the privacy controls in Settings. Exported files remain wherever you choose to save or share them. Disconnecting a calendar removes its cached events; system permission can be revoked in iOS Settings.
- **Subscriptions:** Pro products are being configured. Once available, purchases and cancellations are managed through Apple's App Store account settings. Use Restore purchases with the Apple account used to purchase.
