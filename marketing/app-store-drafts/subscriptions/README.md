# Subscription promotional images

- `planbridge-pro-monthly-1024.png` represents the monthly plan with an open one-month calendar and the label `1 MONTH`.
- `planbridge-pro-annual-1024.png` represents the annual plan with a closed year planner, twelve seasonal markers and the label `1 YEAR`.
- `planbridge-pro-1024.png` is the original shared image retained for history. Do not assign it to both products.

The current assets are opaque RGB PNG files at exactly 1024 × 1024 pixels. Monthly and annual use different compositions, backgrounds and billing-period cues to satisfy App Store Review Guideline 2.3.2.
