==================================================
1. PRODUCT CONCEPT
==================================================

PlanBridge AI is a personal schedule and booking consistency monitor.

It brings together supported information from:

• calendars
• flights
• trains
• hotels
• restaurant reservations
• appointments
• events
• meetings
• travel itineraries
• manually entered bookings
• imported confirmation emails/files

It detects potential conflicts and inconsistencies.

Examples:

“You have dinner booked in Manchester at 19:30, but your train is
scheduled to arrive at 19:42.”

“Your flight leaves Heathrow at 08:15, but your calendar has a meeting
ending at 07:30 in central London.”

“Your hotel check-in begins Monday, but your flight arrives Tuesday.”

“Two appointments overlap by 25 minutes.”

“Your restaurant booking is at 20:00, but your calendar says 19:30.”

“Your return flight has changed, but the corresponding calendar event
still shows the previous departure time.”

The app should answer:

“What conflicts?”

“Why?”

“How serious is it?”

“What information disagrees?”

“What should I check?”

“What changed?”

8. CONFLICT TYPES
==================================================

Detect:

TIME OVERLAP

Two plans occur simultaneously.

Example:

Meeting:
14:00–15:00

Dentist:
14:30

--------------------------------------------------

TRAVEL-TIME CONFLICT

Insufficient time to move between locations.

Example:

Meeting ends:
17:00

Restaurant:
17:15

Travel estimate:
32 minutes

--------------------------------------------------

ARRIVAL CONFLICT

Flight/train arrives after another booking begins.

--------------------------------------------------

DEPARTURE CONFLICT

Another event may leave insufficient time to reach the departure point.

--------------------------------------------------

LOCATION CONFLICT

Two nearby-time events appear in geographically incompatible places.

--------------------------------------------------

DATE MISMATCH

Hotel/transport/event dates disagree.

--------------------------------------------------

TIME MISMATCH

Calendar and booking source show different times.

--------------------------------------------------

DUPLICATE BOOKING

Potential duplicate reservations.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSING CALENDAR ITEM

Booking exists but no corresponding calendar item appears.

--------------------------------------------------

STALE CALENDAR ITEM

Source booking changed but corresponding calendar event appears unchanged.

--------------------------------------------------

CONNECTION RISK

Travel connections have limited scheduled transfer time.

Do not label a connection “impossible” unless deterministic data supports that conclusion.
